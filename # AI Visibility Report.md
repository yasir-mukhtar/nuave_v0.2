## Section 1 — How to read this report (90 seconds)

### How AI answers questions about your business

Think of ChatGPT like a very well-read assistant who:

1. **Reads** hundreds of websites, business listings, news articles, and reviews ahead of time.
2. **Remembers** what it learned.
3. **Answers** your client's question using that memory.

When a bank or fintech asks ChatGPT *"Who should we call for Shariah certification?"* — ChatGPT doesn't call anyone or check anything live. It answers based on everything it has already read about firms like yours on the public internet.

**So your "AI reputation" is simply:** *What ChatGPT has learned about you from the public internet.*

This report shows you: (a) what it learned, (b) why, and (c) what to do about it.

## Section 9 — Your Action Plan

We've ranked every action by **effort ↓** and **impact ↑**. Start at the top and work down.

---

### 🔧 Quick wins (this month)

**🟢 Action 1 — Fix your contact details everywhere. 🆓 Do this first.**

Make your name, phone number, and office address **identical** across every place your business appears online: your website, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile, and any business directories.

- Pick one phone number and use it everywhere.
- Pick one office address format and use it everywhere.
- Pick one registered business name ("Masryef Advisory Sdn Bhd") and use it everywhere.
- Create a Google Business Profile if you don't have one (it's free).

*Why this matters:* Consistency is what tells AI "this is one trusted business" — not several "maybe" businesses with conflicting details. This is the single highest-impact, lowest-effort action in the entire report.

---

**🟢 Action 2 — Add named clients and full scholar profiles to your website. 🆓**

Turn "banks, fintechs, fund managers" into **named, specific case studies** (with client permission). Give each of your three Shariah committee members a full profile page with their qualifications, experience, and publications.

*Why this matters:* AI trusts *named, specific* proof far more than categories. This is the single biggest reason you lose the comparison against Amanie. (See Section 6.)

---

### 📈 The real levers (next 90 days)

**Action 3 — Create content that connects you to broader Shariah advisory topics. 🆓**

Write about sukuk, Takaful, fund management, and Islamic finance conversions — not only fintech. Publish this on your own website and LinkedIn.

*Why this matters:* Right now, you only appear in fintech-specific questions. Broader content makes you visible in general queries too — and when you appear there, you get introduced as "the fintech specialist." (See Section 5.)

---

**Action 4 — Get mentioned outside your own website. 💰**

Write articles for Malaysian and regional fintech and Islamic finance publications. Speak at industry events. Get profiled in "top Shariah advisors in Malaysia" lists and news articles. Aim for mentions on websites that aren't yours.

*Why this matters:* Today, everything AI knows about your reputation comes from your own website. Independent voices — news, listicles, event coverage — are what move AI from "reports what you claim" to "endorses you." (See Sections 7 & 8.)

---

**Action 5 — Keep your website fresh. 🆓**

Update your site monthly with new projects, new awards, and new insights. A website that's actively updated signals to AI that you're a current, active business — and active businesses get recommended more often than static ones.

*Why this matters:* AI treats regularly updated businesses as more relevant and trustworthy than businesses whose websites haven't changed in years.

---

**Action 6 — Collect and publish proof from your clients. 🆓**

Gather testimonials, short written case studies, and before-and-after results (e.g., "helped X fintech get Shariah-certified in 6 weeks"). Publish these on your website and encourage clients to mention you on their own sites and LinkedIn.

*Why this matters:* In AI's eyes, a client saying "Masryef did great work for us" is worth more than anything you say about yourself. Third-party proof is the strongest signal of all. (See Section 7.)

---

- **Google Business Profile dashboard** — once you create or claim your profile, Google gives you simple tips to improve it.
- **Your LinkedIn company page** — post your awards, new clients, and insights regularly. LinkedIn content is heavily read by AI.
- **Malaysian Islamic finance publications and listicles** — getting listed on even one "top Shariah advisor" article is a trusted signal that's independent of your own website.
