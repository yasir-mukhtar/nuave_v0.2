# 06 — Audit Report

> Status: Working product plan and content sample
> Depends on: [`04 - Questions.md`](./04%20-%20Questions.md) and
> [`05 - Audit Run.md`](./05%20-%20Audit%20Run.md)
> Updated: 2026-08-17

## Objective

Turn ten customer-approved observations into a report a non-technical business
decision-maker can understand in about ten minutes, trust as a truthful record,
share as a PDF, and use to choose concrete work.

The intended customer reaction is:

> “I can see what happened when my questions were tested, what matters, and
> what my team should do next. The evidence is here, and Nuave has not made the
> result sound more certain than it is.”

This plan settles report content and evidence hierarchy only. Visual design,
component layout, PDF art direction, and the complete Indonesian writing
contract are separate work.

## Settled content decisions

1. The report follows the exact ten questions the customer approved, including
   their final order and wording.
2. The final question composition uses **Tanpa menyebut bisnis Anda** and
   **Menyebut bisnis Anda**. Original suggestion categories are not presented
   as factual after substantial customer edits.
3. Direct counts with visible denominators lead the result. An empty denominator
   is **Tidak diuji**, not zero performance.
4. The report contains one to five evidence-backed actions. If the evidence
   cannot support at least one useful action, the report fails review rather
   than receiving filler.
5. The primary artifact action is **Download PDF** when the PDF is ready.
   Evidence export is secondary. PDF failure does not withhold the validated web
   report.
6. The screen report and PDF use the same report record and version. Different
   layouts may not create different interpretations.
7. The report is delivered only after ten of ten observations are evaluable.
   Completion is not treated as positive performance.
8. The report separates observation, interpretation, and action.
9. Other named businesses are described as **other businesses mentioned**
   unless verified context supports calling them competitors.
10. Final Indonesian report language receives a dedicated review later. The
    English sample below tests content and evidence, not final customer voice.
11. The Nuave score is the direct overall appearance count out of ten, for
    example **4/10**. Separate **Tanpa menyebut bisnis Anda** and **Menyebut
    bisnis Anda** measures remain directly beneath it.
12. This module owns report-ready customer email, email-delivery failure, and
    resend. A later access-and-recovery module owns opening private access,
    another-device return, and expired or revoked access.

## Result and denominator contract

| Result | Numerator | Denominator | No eligible question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overall Nuave score | Retained answers where the resolved business visibly appeared | All ten evaluable observations | Not applicable; delivery requires 10/10 |
| Appearance without the business name | Evaluable answers where the resolved business visibly appeared | Final questions classified **Tanpa menyebut bisnis Anda** | **Tidak diuji** |
| Recognition when the business is named | Evaluable answers where the resolved business visibly appeared | Final questions classified **Menyebut bisnis Anda** | **Tidak diuji** |
| Recommendation | Supported `recommended` results | Questions where recommendation was meaningfully assessed | **Tidak diuji** |
| Comparison | Supported comparison results | Questions where comparison was meaningfully assessed | **Tidak diuji** |
| Public information | Confirmed, incomplete, or conflicting results | Questions where business information was meaningfully assessed | **Tidak diuji** |

A question may be evaluable while one or more result dimensions remain not
assessed. A factual address question, for example, must not become a failed
recommendation merely because it did not recommend the business.

The overall score counts answers in which the resolved business visibly
appeared. It does not convert the result to a percentage, rank, or forecast.
Because questions that name the business test recognition rather than
spontaneous discovery, the report must keep the two composition measures
separate beneath the overall count.

## Content hierarchy

### Before the main content

- audited business and exact branch, area, or service scope;
- audit date;
- ten-question scope;
- ordering business and named recipient; and
- primary **Download PDF** action.

### 1. Main result

- one overall appearance count out of ten and its direct score;
- one headline count for **Tanpa menyebut bisnis Anda**;
- one separate recognition count for **Menyebut bisnis Anda**;
- recommendation, comparison, and public-information measures only with their
  eligible denominators;
- one short conclusion; and
- the snapshot limitation beside the result.

### 2. Key findings

Use one to five material findings. One or two strong findings are deliverable.
Each one includes:

- **Observed:** the answer or public evidence showed;
- **Interpretation:** what the pattern may mean, without claiming cause; and
- **Evidence:** exact test references.

Do not create a finding merely to fill a section.

When other businesses materially appear, finish this section with **Other
businesses mentioned**. Show the name, observed relationship, number of
relevant tests, and test references. Do not infer market share, stable rank,
quality, or business performance.

### 3. What to do next

Use one to five ordered actions. Each action includes:

- the concrete work;
- why it matters;
- evidence references;
- a suggested owner;
- an observable completion check; and
- a caveat when the action could be mistaken for a guarantee.

When no immediate corrective gap is supported, use a truthful maintenance or
further-investigation action: preserve a supported strength, improve the public
evidence behind it, or check an explicitly untested aspect. Do not present an
untested aspect as an observed weakness.

### 4. Test-by-test results

Preserve all ten questions in approved order. Each entry includes:

- **Tanpa menyebut bisnis Anda** or **Menyebut bisnis Anda**;
- exact question;
- plain-language result status;
- exact answer excerpt;
- full answer available on the web report;
- sources used for the displayed claim;
- observation date and context; and
- a correction note when public information conflicts.

### 5. How this audit works

Keep provider and model mechanics low in the hierarchy. Build this section from
recorded run facts, including the exact surface, returned model, language,
location when used, date range, web-search condition, retries, and method
version. State what the audit does not prove and provide the correction path.

### 2. Key findings

#### Finding 1 — The business appeared in half of the questions without its name

**Observed:** Kopi Taman Senja appeared in tests 03, 04, and 05. It did not
appear in tests 01, 02, and 06.

**Interpretation:** The business was visible for some Dago work-and-coffee
needs, but the tested answers did not identify it consistently across closely
related requests.

**Evidence:** Tests 01–06.

#### Finding 2 — One question without the name recommended the business

**Observed:** Test 04 proposed Kopi Taman Senja for local coffee and working in
Bandung. Tests 03 and 05 mentioned it without making it the preferred option.

**Interpretation:** Appearance and recommendation were not the same in these
answers. One supported recommendation should not be presented as a stable rank.

**Evidence:** Tests 03–05.

#### Finding 3 — Published opening hours conflicted

**Observed:** Test 08 returned 08:00–22:00 from the fictional official site and
09:00–22:00 from a fictional directory.

**Interpretation:** A customer checking opening time may receive different
answers depending on which public source is used.

**Evidence:** Test 08.

#### Finding 4 — Meeting information was incomplete

**Observed:** Tests 02, 06, and 09 did not find one official page confirming
room capacity, reservation rules, power outlets, and Wi-Fi availability.

**Interpretation:** The tested system could identify the cafe, but it could not
answer all practical meeting questions from one authoritative source.

**Evidence:** Tests 02, 06, and 09.

### 3. What to do next

#### 1. Make the opening hours consistent

**Do:** Publish the same opening hours on the official site and priority public
business profiles.  
**Why:** Test 08 found two different opening times.  
**Owner:** Business owner or admin.  
**Done when:** Every controlled public profile shows the same hours and the
business has checked each live page.  
**Caveat:** Consistent information does not guarantee a future AI answer.

#### 2. Publish one complete work-and-meeting facilities page

**Do:** State seating capacity, reservation rules, Wi-Fi, power outlets, food,
drinks, and any time limits on one official page.  
**Why:** Tests 02, 06, and 09 could not confirm these details from one official
source.  
**Owner:** Marketing.  
**Done when:** The official page answers each tested facilities question and is
linked from the main navigation or business profile.  
**Caveat:** This improves public clarity; it does not prove why the business was
absent from an answer.

#### 3. Make the reservation path explicit

**Do:** Add one current reservation method with the expected response channel
and the information a customer should provide.  
**Why:** Test 10 found contact details but no clear meeting-reservation process.  
**Owner:** Admin.  
**Done when:** A customer can move from the official page to the stated
reservation channel without searching elsewhere.

#### 4. Describe the relevant use cases with verified facts

**Do:** Add a concise official description of when the cafe is suitable for
working, a small meeting, or local-coffee discovery.  
**Why:** The business did not appear in three of six questions without its
name, while the answers that did mention it used those specific use cases.  
**Owner:** Marketing.  
**Done when:** The official description states only facilities and uses the
business can verify, with no unsupported superiority claim.  
**Caveat:** The observed absence does not establish that missing copy caused
the result.

### 5. How this audit works

Nuave tested the ten approved questions separately with web search on 17 August
2026. Six final questions did not name Kopi Taman Senja and four did. The
report counts only visible appearances in the retained answers; a citation URL
alone does not count. Recommendation, comparison, and public-information
results use only questions where those dimensions were assessed.

This is a recorded snapshot, not every possible consumer-interface answer.
The final customer report will disclose the exact execution surface, returned
model, language, location context, method version, and retry history from the
saved run record.

If the business identity, an official fact, or Nuave's interpretation appears
wrong, the recipient can report the problem to `support@nuave.ai`. If the
founder approves an objectively verifiable correction, Nuave preserves the
original evidence and creates a new report version rather than silently
rewriting the delivered report. A customer who selected the wrong business
uses the replacement-audit remedy instead; that is not a report correction.

## Report-ready email and delivery ownership

Send the report-ready email through Resend as
**Tim Nuave <support@nuave.ai>** after the web report has passed evidence and
writing checks and the approved private-access destination exists. PDF
generation is a separate derived-artifact state and does not block this email.
The email contains:

- the exact audited business and report date;
- a concise statement that the report is ready;
- the approved private report-access action;
- the current PDF status without promising a download that is not ready;
- **Download PDF** as the primary report action on the report itself when the
  artifact is ready;
- the snapshot limitation and finite-access wording approved for delivery; and
- one help route for a report or delivery problem.

Create one idempotent delivery record per report version, recipient version,
and email-template version. A duplicate report-ready event cannot send a
notification storm. A resend creates a new delivery attempt against the same
immutable report; it never regenerates the report or reruns an observation.

Track the report artifacts separately:

- `web_report_ready` — the complete validated report may be delivered;
- `pdf_pending` — PDF generation is still running;
- `pdf_ready` — **Download PDF** is available; and
- `pdf_failed` — the web report remains delivered while Nuave retries or
  troubleshoots the PDF from the same report version.

When the PDF is pending or failed, keep the locked **Download PDF** label visible
but unavailable with a truthful nearby status. Do not create a second report,
change facts, or rerun observations. When the PDF succeeds, the same action
becomes available without changing the report version.

Customer-visible delivery failure:

> **Email laporan belum berhasil dikirim**
>
> Laporan Anda sudah siap dan tidak perlu dibuat ulang. Nuave akan mencoba
> mengirimkannya kembali.
>
> `[Kirim ulang email]` `[Minta bantuan]`

The selected later access-and-recovery module—not this content plan—owns opening
private report access, another-device return, lost access, and expired or
revoked access. This plan does not assume that module is an account or
dashboard.

## Acceptance criteria for the content plan

1. The overall headline counts visible appearance across all ten retained
   answers and displays the matching score out of ten.
2. Appearance in **Tanpa menyebut bisnis Anda** and recognition in **Menyebut
   bisnis Anda** remain directly beneath it with their own denominators.
3. Recommendation, comparison, and information use only assessed denominators.
4. A zero eligible denominator is **Tidak diuji**.
5. The report contains one to five evidence-backed actions with owners and
   completion checks.
6. No original suggestion category is presented as factual after substantial
   editing.
7. Every material claim and action references retained evidence.
8. Other businesses are not described as competitors or ranked without
   supporting context.
9. Ten exact questions and evidence details remain available in approved order.
10. **Download PDF** is the primary report action.
11. Screen and PDF use the same report version and facts.
12. The sample remains clearly fictional and cannot be mistaken for customer
    proof.
13. Indonesian-language approval is not inferred from this English content
    sample.
14. The overall Nuave score is the visible appearance count out of ten and its
    arithmetic matches the retained answers.
15. The report contains one to five material findings, with one or two strong
    findings accepted without filler.
16. A maintenance or further-investigation action is explicitly labelled and
    never presents an untested aspect as an observed weakness.
17. Report-ready email is emitted only for a validated report and duplicate
    events are idempotent.
18. Delivery retry and resend reuse the same report version and never rerun the
    report or audit.
19. A validated web report and report-ready email can be delivered while PDF is
    pending or failed.
20. A later successful PDF renders the identical report version and restores
    the **Download PDF** action without changing report facts.
21. Report-ready and support email uses Resend as
    **Tim Nuave <support@nuave.ai>**.

## Known implementation gaps

- The current report schema allows at most three priorities; it must support
  one to five.
- The current report screen still uses original English intent categories and
  copy.
- Recommendation is generally forced into assessed states for every completed
  question; factual questions need a protected `not assessed` path.
- Comparison and information summaries do not yet expose their eligible
  denominators consistently.
- The current primary toolbar action is JSON export rather than **Download
  PDF**.
- The current screen contains agency-era attribution and does not render a
  dedicated other-business evidence section.
- The current implementation does not yet compute and display the direct
  appearance count out of ten with the required name/no-name breakdown.
- Report-ready customer email, delivery records, and resend are not yet
  implemented.
- Web-report and PDF-artifact readiness are not yet represented separately.
