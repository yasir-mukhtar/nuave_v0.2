### 3. What to do next

#### 1. Make the opening hours consistent

**Do:** Publish the same opening hours on the official site and priority public
business profiles.  
**Why:** Test 08 found two different opening times.  
**Owner:** Business owner or admin.  
**Done when:** Every controlled public profile shows the same hours and the
business has checked each live page.  
**Caveat:** Consistent information does not guarantee a future AI answer.

#### 2. Publish one complete work-and-meeting facilities page

**Do:** State seating capacity, reservation rules, Wi-Fi, power outlets, food,
drinks, and any time limits on one official page.  
**Why:** Tests 02, 06, and 09 could not confirm these details from one official
source.  
**Owner:** Marketing.  
**Done when:** The official page answers each tested facilities question and is
linked from the main navigation or business profile.  
**Caveat:** This improves public clarity; it does not prove why the business was
absent from an answer.

#### 3. Make the reservation path explicit

**Do:** Add one current reservation method with the expected response channel
and the information a customer should provide.  
**Why:** Test 10 found contact details but no clear meeting-reservation process.  
**Owner:** Admin.  
**Done when:** A customer can move from the official page to the stated
reservation channel without searching elsewhere.

#### 4. Describe the relevant use cases with verified facts

**Do:** Add a concise official description of when the cafe is suitable for
working, a small meeting, or local-coffee discovery.  
**Why:** The business did not appear in three of six questions without its
name, while the answers that did mention it used those specific use cases.  
**Owner:** Marketing.  
**Done when:** The official description states only facilities and uses the
business can verify, with no unsupported superiority claim.  
**Caveat:** The observed absence does not establish that missing copy caused
the result.
