## 1. The founder's direction (what we are solving)

The current landing page is too thin for this market. Clinic owners and their
marketing teams mostly do not know yet that patients now ask AI for clinic
recommendations. The page must **educate first, then sell**:

1. The new behaviour: patients ask AI (ChatGPT, Google's AI answers) which
   clinic to choose.
2. Why they should care: these questions carry real, strong intent. The person
   asking is close to booking.
3. It is a new field. Most clinics have never looked at it.
4. Why ChatGPT and Google AI Overview in particular matter.
5. How being named in AI answers connects to more bookings (the mechanism,
   never a promise).
6. Anything else that moves a clinic owner from "never heard of this" to
   "I need to check my clinic today".

Persuasion: use NLP (neuro-linguistic programming) language patterns, applied
honestly. Craft bar: it must read like a senior Indonesian copywriter at the
level of Gojek's team wrote it (human, local, crafted), **without** copying
Gojek's playful brand voice. Method: absorb Harry Dry's Marketing Examples
landing-page guide (section 7 below).

## 2. Product truth the page must not bend

What Nuave is, in plain words:

- Nuave asks AI **10 questions in Indonesian**, the way patients would ask,
  and shows whether the clinic was **named** and whether it was
  **recommended**, which other clinics were named, what AI said, the sources
  AI used, and what to fix first.
- Tested system: **AI built by OpenAI, the maker of ChatGPT**, with web
  search. In marketing copy say "AI buatan OpenAI, pembuat ChatGPT". Never call
  Nuave's answers "jawaban ChatGPT". Nuave does **not** test Google AI
  Overview, Gemini, Perplexity or Claude. The page may *educate* about Google
  AI Overview, but must never claim Nuave measures it.
- Journey today: enter the clinic's website or Instagram → **free check**
  (Nuave finds the clinic's name, logo and description; this is *not* an AI
  result) → order with an email → pay **Rp99.000** (QRIS, transfer bank,
  GoPay, DANA via Midtrans; total, no tax or fee added; one-time, no
  subscription) → Nuave reads the website and prepares the clinic's details →
  the owner checks the details (services, intended patients, what sets them
  apart, competitors, areas) → Nuave suggests 10 questions, the owner can edit
  them → the audit runs on the server (the tab can be closed) → the report
  arrives by a private email link from Tim Nuave (support@nuave.ai).
- The first live order was delivered about 10.5 minutes after the questions
  were approved (13 October 2026). That is internal evidence, **not** an
  approved delivery promise. Any delivery time on the page is a
  `[KONFIRMASI]` placeholder.
- The report shows, in this order: **Hasil singkat** (the exact labels
  "Bisnis Anda muncul di X dari 10 pertanyaan" and "Bisnis Anda
  direkomendasikan di Y dari 10 pertanyaan"), **Jawaban model AI** (all ten
  questions and full answers, with sources), **Analisis Nuave**, **Yang dapat
  dilakukan** (one to ten actions tied to evidence), **Tentang audit ini**
  (method and limits). Web page plus **Download PDF**. Other businesses named
  are "bisnis lain yang disebut". A re-check after 6 to 8 weeks is
  recommended.
- No patient data is needed or collected. Nuave uses only public information
  about the clinic. It does not judge clinical quality.
- Refunds: the live terms do not yet promise refunds; milestone S5 will. A
  refund line on the page is a `[KONFIRMASI S5]` placeholder (draft terms
  propose a full refund if cancelled before the audit starts).
- There are **no customers, testimonials, logos, ratings or results** we can
  show yet. None may be invented. A sample report must use a fictional clinic
  and say so.

## 3. Who we are talking to

Two readers, one page.

**A. The clinic owner** (often a dentist or doctor who also runs the
business, or a business owner who employs doctors). Dental, aesthetic and skin
clinics first; also physiotherapy, child-development, eye and similar private
clinics. One location or a small chain. Reads on a phone between patients or
at night. Practical, proud of the clinic's quality, sceptical of marketing
hype and "digital" salespeople, careful about medical advertising rules. Cares
about: new patients every week, the clinic's name and reputation, the clinic
across the road and the big chains, not wasting money.

**B. The clinic marketing team** (a marketing lead, a social media admin, an
agency contact). Runs Instagram, Google Maps profile, reviews, the website,
ads, WhatsApp admin. Wants new levers, has to report to the owner, and needs
something concrete to show in a meeting. Quiet fear: being the last to know
about a new channel. Wants to look sharp, not behind.

**Awareness level:** mostly *unaware* to *problem-aware* (Eugene Schwartz).
They know Google and Instagram. They have not seen how AI answers a patient's
question about their area. So the page opens with the scene and the tension,
not with the product. Harry Dry: for cold traffic, lead with the problem.

**How people arrive** (the growth marketer will refine this):

1. *Outreach*: Nuave's sales motion runs a few unnamed questions about a
   clinic before contact and leads with what was observed. These visitors
   already saw one finding about their clinic. They need trust, the full
   picture and a fast path to the form.
2. *Social and content* (Instagram, LinkedIn, WhatsApp shares between clinic
   owners): cold, curious. They need the education.
3. *A bounded paid test* later (Meta or Google ads): cold, impatient.
4. *Referral from an agency or a fellow owner*: warm.

## 4. The big idea and the story of the page

**Big idea (working):** *Patients no longer search. They ask. And AI answers
with names.* The question for every clinic: **is your clinic one of those
names?**

**The tension only we can say** (Harry Dry: "can nobody else say this?"):
clinics are not allowed to advertise themselves as the best or compare
themselves with other clinics (Permenkes 1787/2010, see F6). Patients still
ask AI "klinik gigi mana yang bagus di Depok?". AI answers, compares and
recommends anyway, from whatever public information it finds. The clinic does
not write that answer. It can only make sure the information AI reads is
clear and correct. Use this carefully: it is an observation about the
situation, not legal advice and not a way around the rule.

**Narrative arc (skeleton v0).** The copywriter may merge, split or reorder,
but must say why.

| # | Section | Job of the section | Main technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Top bar | Logo, "Contoh laporan", one CTA | — |
| 1 | Hero | Stop the scroll with the tension; say what Nuave does; website field and CTA | Hook title, call-to-value CTA, objection microcopy |
| 2 | "You've done the right things" | Pace the reader: Maps, reviews, Instagram, website, ads. Then lead: there is one new place patients ask | Pacing and leading, yes-set |
| 3 | The new behaviour | From a keyword ("klinik gigi depok") to a full question with needs, area, budget, time; AI answers with a short list | Show, don't tell; sensory scene |
| 4 | Why it matters: intent | The question carries the whole decision; being named means being considered; people click less when AI answers (F3) | Reframe, chunk down to one patient |
| 5 | ChatGPT and Google AI Overview | Two doors to the same answer: ChatGPT (health questions at huge scale, F4) and Google's AI answer on top of search in Indonesia (F1, F2) | Presupposition, evidence |

## 6. NLP playbook, applied honestly

Use these patterns where they make the reader *understand faster or feel the
situation more clearly*. Never use them to create fear, false urgency or a
belief that is not true.

| Pattern | What it does | Where it fits |
|---|---|---|
| Pacing and leading | Say three true things the reader already agrees with, then lead to the new idea | Section 2 ("Anda sudah punya Google Maps. Ulasan Anda bagus. Instagram rutin. Lalu ada satu tempat baru...") |
| Presupposition | Phrase the new behaviour as a given, when it is true | "Saat pasien Anda bertanya ke AI..." (not "Jika...") |
| Sensory, specific language (VAK) | Let them *see* the chat, *hear* the patient's question, *feel* the moment of choosing | Section 3, the hero visual |
| Future pacing | Let them imagine a concrete future moment | Sections 9 and 15 ("Saat pasien berikutnya bertanya ke AI, Anda sudah tahu jawabannya.") |
| Reframing | Change the frame: from "AI is a tech topic" to "this is about who gets chosen" | Sections 4 and 6 |
| Chunking down | From "the AI era" to one patient, one question, one answer, one name | Sections 3 and 4 |
| Towards and away-from motivation | Speak to both: being chosen (towards) and patients going to the clinic next door (away-from), without fear-mongering | Hero and section 4 |
| Embedded command | A soft instruction inside a sentence | "...sebelum Anda *cek klinik Anda* hari ini" |
| Identity level (Dilts) | Speak to who they are: careful clinicians who care how patients find them | Founder's note, section 13 |
| "Karena" (reason why) | People comply more when given a reason | CTA microcopy, price |
| Anchoring | Put Rp99.000 next to an honest, familiar comparison | Section 12 |

Ethical lines: no countdowns, no scarcity, no "jangan sampai tertinggal", no
invented numbers, no promise of patients, revenue, rankings or "selalu
direkomendasikan".

## 7. Marketing Examples playbook (Harry Dry), absorbed

**Page order (his 10 steps):** title (value) → subtitle (how) → visual (show
the real product in use) → social proof for believability → CTA → features and
objections → social proof that inspires action → FAQ → second CTA (remind
why) → founder's note. We have no social proof, so believability comes from
showing the real report format, method transparency, real sources and
candour.

**Titles:** speak with conviction ("It's how", not "We help"). No fancy, empty
lines. Interesting in six words, or lead with the benefit. Cold readers: name
the problem. Write ten titles, pick the most memorable, not the most liked.
Never a "contained" title: it must pull the reader down.

**CTA:** call to value, not "Daftar". Add a few words that answer the main
hesitation. The website field plus button in the hero reduces friction
(collect everything else later). Add warmth.

**Copy rules:** write with an eraser. Don't exaggerate. Talk about what it
does for them. Active voice. No landing-page words (in Indonesian:
"optimalkan", "tingkatkan", "maksimalkan", "solusi terdepan", "ekosistem",
"revolusioner", "era digital"). Find the tension. Write how people talk. More
full stops, fewer commas. Kill adjectives and adverbs. Take a stance. The
first line is short. Every line pulls into the next (slippery slide). Copy is
selling, not decoration.

**Three tests for every line:** Can I *visualise* it? Can I *falsify* it? Can
*nobody else* say it? Three no's means rewrite.

**Conversational:** use the reader's own words (patients, jadwal praktik,
klinik sebelah, admin WhatsApp, ulasan Google Maps). Pronouns. Stories over
lists. Empathise. Respect the competition. Read it aloud at the table; if it
makes someone cringe, rewrite.

**Final test:** would this line help sell if Yasir were sitting across the
table from a clinic owner? If not, cut it.

## 8. Voice and language rules (from `docs/VOICE.md`, adapted for clinics)

- Indonesian, addressed with **Anda**. Never "kamu", "Bapak/Ibu" or
  "pengguna".
- Because this page is only for clinics: say **klinik Anda** and **pasien /
  calon pasien** (VOICE.md allows the business type when it is known).
- Plain **AI** in marketing copy. "ChatGPT" only for the patient's habit and
  for what AI does, never for Nuave's own answers. Name the maker near any
  claim about what Nuave tests: "AI buatan OpenAI, pembuat ChatGPT".
- **No em dashes or en dashes in prose.** Commas, full stops, "sampai".
- Short sentences, one idea each. Think in Indonesian first; no translated
  English syntax.
- Light spoken words are welcome where a person would say them: cuma, kok,
  duluan, kasih tahu, biar, ya, nggak (sparingly, still with Anda).
- Exact settled labels stay exact: "Bisnis Anda muncul di X dari 10
  pertanyaan", "Bisnis Anda direkomendasikan di Y dari 10 pertanyaan",
  "Download PDF", "AI Visibility Report", "Rp99.000".
- Settled CTA is "Cek bisnis saya di AI". For this clinic page the team may
  propose "Cek klinik saya di AI" as the main CTA; the settled one stays as
  the control for an A/B test. Flag it for the founder.
- Prefer "pesaing" (never "kompetitor"), "temuan" (never "insight"), "cek
  ulang" (never "monitoring").
- Avoid jargon (GEO, AEO, LLM, SEO terms) or explain it in plain words the
  first time.
- Fictional examples are labelled "Contoh tampilan. Nama klinik fiktif."
  Never use a real clinic's name or logo. Other clinics in a mock answer are
  "Klinik A", "Klinik B" or blurred.
- No medical claims, no claims about any clinic's quality of care.

## 9. The craft bar: "a senior Gojek copywriter wrote this"

Not Gojek's voice. Gojek's *craft*: it sounds like a real person from here
who understands the reader's day, picks the one detail that makes you nod, and
says it in fewer words than you expected.

| Feels translated or corporate | Feels human and local |
|---|---|
| Optimalkan visibilitas klinik Anda di era kecerdasan buatan. | Pasien sekarang nanya ke AI dulu, baru datang ke klinik. |
| Nuave adalah solusi audit visibilitas AI yang komprehensif. | Kami bertanya ke AI seperti pasien Anda bertanya. Lalu kami tunjukkan jawabannya. |
| Tingkatkan peluang klinik Anda untuk direkomendasikan. | Klinik Anda disebut, atau klinik sebelah? |
| Dapatkan insight yang actionable. | Anda tahu apa yang harus dibenahi duluan. |

Signs we are there: a clinic owner reads the hero aloud and it sounds like
something a sharp friend would say over coffee; every section has one detail
only a clinic person would recognise (jam praktik dokter, "bisa cicil?", admin
WhatsApp yang balasnya lama, ulasan bintang lima yang tidak dibaca AI); there
is rhythm (short, short, longer); wit appears at most a few times and never
undercuts trust.
