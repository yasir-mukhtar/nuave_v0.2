# Clinic landing page: content strategy v2 (light)

> Status: approved direction (Yasir, 24 October 2026: "go with your recommendations"). It replaces [`STRATEGY.md`](./STRATEGY.md) v1, kept as history. Final copy: [`COPY-v2.md`](./COPY-v2.md). Lofi: [`LOFI-v2.md`](./LOFI-v2.md) and the Figma page "Lofi v2 · Light".
> Why v2: Yasir found v1 too heavy. It looked like a typical local digital-marketer page: wordy, over-explained, a little scammy, and likely to bounce. His reference for the right weight is [haloai.co.id](https://www.haloai.co.id/) (not to copy; we are a different product).

## 1. What went wrong in v1, in plain words

v1 explained every step of the argument. It was the right content in the wrong place.

| | v1 (our page) | HaloAI |
|---|---|---|
| Sections | 17, plus sticky bar | 10, plus FAQ |
| Words a visitor reads | about 2,200, not counting the mock screens | about 600 outside the FAQ and the chat demos |
| Length | about 22 phone screens | about 11 desktop screens; mostly pictures |
| A typical section | label, headline, two or three paragraphs, a diagram, a source line, a closing line | one-word label, a headline of 5 to 8 words, at most one line, then a product picture |
| How it explains | tells the reasoning (keyword vs question, anatomy of a question, two doors, the path to booking) | shows the product working (a live chat demo, real screens) |
| Depth | always open | behind tabs and the FAQ |
| Tone | many persuasion moves stacked: questions, "bayangkan", checklists, struck-through ads, many disclaimers | calm, confident statements |

Two things made it feel scammy. One was too much persuasion: every section tried to convince. The other was too much fine print: source lines, `[KONFIRMASI]` boxes and limits everywhere. A confident product says less, and shows more.

## 2. The 20% that carries 80% of the understanding

A clinic owner needs only six ideas to say yes. Everything else is cut, moved to the FAQ, or shown inside a picture.

| # | The idea | How the page delivers it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Patients now ask AI before choosing a clinic. | The hero headline, said as a fact |
| 2 | AI shapes their choice, and they trust it. | One row of three big numbers |
| 3 | AI names only a few clinics, and you can't see whether yours is one. | One short section with a side-by-side picture |
| 4 | Nuave asks AI 10 questions the way your patients do. | Three steps |
| 5 | You get your score, what AI says about you, the other clinics it names, and what to fix first. | The report, shown in tabs |
| 6 | Rp99.000, once. You pay after we find your clinic. | Hero microcopy and the price card |

## 3. Weight rules for v2

1. **Eight sections plus a footer.** Target: about 9 phone screens on mobile and about 6 on desktop.
2. **No small text above titles.** If a label helps navigation, it is one word and matches the menu: *Cara kerja*, *Laporan*, *Harga*, *FAQ*. The hero has none.
3. **Each headline is 8 words or fewer, with at most one line under it (15 words or fewer).** Cards have a title of one or two words and a line of 10 words or fewer.
4. **No paragraphs outside the FAQ.** FAQ answers are two sentences at most.
5. **State the conclusion; let a picture or the FAQ hold the reasoning.**
6. **One number per card.** Sources go in one small line per section, not under every claim. Founder placeholders (`[KONFIRMASI]`) live in this document, never on the page.
7. **Two pictures do the teaching.** An AI chat demo with clinic-type tabs (*Gigi · Kecantikan · Kulit · Anak*), and the report shown in tabs.
8. **Persuasion stays light.** One confident presupposition (the hero), concrete examples (inside the demo), one reason why ("Bayar setelah klinik Anda kami temukan") and one look ahead (the final headline). No stacked questions, no "bayangkan", no jabs at the clinic next door.
9. **Honesty does not change.** No invented numbers, no promises of patients, revenue or rankings. Disclosures move to where people look for them (the FAQ and the report), but nothing is removed.
10. **v1's education becomes content marketing.** The deeper explanations go to outreach messages, Instagram and LinkedIn posts and short articles: the keyword-vs-question shift, the advertising-rule tension, what AI reads, the path from answer to booking. Those channels bring people in; the landing page is where they decide.

## 5. Draft copy (Indonesian)

> This is the draft Yasir approved in direction. The final wording is in [`COPY-v2.md`](./COPY-v2.md): the copywriter polished it and folded in the growth marketer's eight must-fixes. The hero microcopy now says the first step is free; step 2 shows the owner's own checking; each number card names its scope; the report headline no longer implies a ranking; the final headline no longer promises what one snapshot can't; and there's a new FAQ on changing answers. The draft below is kept for the record.

This is about 400 words a visitor reads, counting the menu, the demo and every repeat of the button, plus FAQ answers they open on demand. v1 had about 2,200 before its mock screens. The copywriter polishes it when the lofi is updated.

### 1. Hero

- **H1:** Pasien sekarang tanya AI dulu, baru pilih klinik.
- **Sub:** Nuave menunjukkan apakah AI merekomendasikan klinik Anda, dan apa yang perlu dibenahi.
- **Field:** Website atau Instagram klinik
- **Button:** Cek klinik saya di AI
- **Under the button:** Rp99.000 sekali bayar. Bayar setelah klinik Anda kami temukan.
- **Three facts:** 10 pertanyaan seperti pasien · AI buatan OpenAI · Tanpa data pasien
- **Demo:**
  - Tabs: *Gigi · Kecantikan · Kulit · Anak*.
  - Each tab shows one patient question, an AI answer naming three fictional clinics (*Klinik A, B, C*, one reason each), and an empty slot: **Klinik Anda?**
  - Example (Gigi): *"Klinik gigi di Depok yang bisa cicil behel dan buka Sabtu, ada rekomendasi?"*
  - Tiny caption: *Contoh. Nama klinik fiktif.*

Alternative H1s to test:
- *Lihat apa kata AI tentang klinik Anda.* This says what Nuave is.
- *Klinik Anda direkomendasikan AI?*

v1's *"Klinik Anda disebut AI, atau klinik sebelah?"* is not recommended. The jab at the clinic next door is part of what felt scammy.

### 2. Why now

- **H2:** AI sekarang jadi rujukan pasien.
- **Line:** Jawaban AI kini ikut menentukan dokter dan klinik yang mereka pilih.
- **Cards:**
  - **47%**: pasien di AS sudah mencari dokter lewat AI.
  - **40 juta**: orang bertanya soal kesehatan ke ChatGPT setiap hari.
  - **2024**: Google mulai menjawab dengan AI di Indonesia.
- **Source line:** *Sumber: rater8 Patient Choice Report 2026, survei 992 orang dewasa di AS; OpenAI, Januari 2026; Google Indonesia, 2024.*

"Rujukan" is the word clinics use for a doctor's referral. The rater8 report says AI now shapes patient choice more than Google search or a doctor's referral, which is what the line under the headline states.

### 3. The blind spot

- **H2:** AI cuma menyebut beberapa klinik.
- **Line:** Klinik Anda disebut atau tidak, Anda tidak bisa melihatnya.
- **Picture labels:** *Yang dilihat pasien* · *Yang dilihat klinik* ("Belum ada data jawaban AI")

### 4. How it works (label: Cara kerja)

- **H2:** Kami bertanya ke AI seperti pasien Anda.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Masukkan website.** Kami temukan klinik Anda. Gratis.
  2. **Kami tanya AI.** 10 pertanyaan seperti pasien, tanpa menyebut nama klinik Anda. *(tag: Rp99.000)*
  3. **Terima laporan.** Link-nya dikirim ke email Anda, bisa diunduh sebagai PDF.

### 5. Report (label: Laporan)

- **H2:** Lihat posisi klinik Anda. Tahu apa yang dibenahi.
- **Tabs** (each tab shows that part of the real report, with a fictional clinic):
  - **Skor:** Muncul di berapa dari 10 pertanyaan, dan direkomendasikan di berapa.
  - **Jawaban AI:** Apa kata AI tentang klinik Anda, lengkap dengan sumbernya.
  - **Klinik lain:** Yang disebut AI, dan kapan mereka diunggulkan.
  - **Perbaikan:** Langkah yang dikerjakan duluan, dan siapa yang sebaiknya mengerjakan.
- **Under the tabs:** Download PDF, siap dibawa ke rapat.

### 6. Why Nuave

- **H2:** Bukti, bukan janji.
- **Cards:**
  - **Apa adanya:** Jawaban AI ditampilkan utuh, dengan sumbernya.
  - **Bahasa pasien:** 10 pertanyaan dalam Bahasa Indonesia, seperti pasien bertanya.
  - **Tanpa data pasien:** Kami hanya membaca informasi publik klinik.
  - **Jujur:** Bukan peringkat, bukan janji pasien. Bisa dicek ulang.

### 7. Price (label: Harga)

- **H2:** Rp99.000. Sekali bayar.
- **Line:** Bandingkan dengan harga satu tindakan di klinik Anda.
- **Card:**
  - ✓ 10 pertanyaan seperti pasien · ✓ Jawaban AI dengan sumbernya · ✓ Klinik lain yang disebut · ✓ Daftar perbaikan dan PDF
  - Form and button, with **Bayar setelah klinik Anda kami temukan.** above it
  - Chips: QRIS · Transfer bank · GoPay · DANA
  - **Tanpa langganan. Tanpa biaya tambahan.**

### 8. FAQ (label: FAQ)

- **H2:** Yang sering ditanyakan
- **Bedanya dengan SEO?** SEO soal posisi website di Google. Nuave melihat isi jawaban AI: klinik Anda disebut atau tidak, dan dari sumber apa.
- **Bisa cek sendiri di ChatGPT?** Bisa, untuk satu pertanyaan. Nuave mengajukan 10 pertanyaan seperti pasien, menyimpan semua jawaban dan sumbernya, lalu menuliskan apa yang dibenahi.
- **AI apa yang diuji?** AI buatan OpenAI, pembuat ChatGPT, dengan pencarian web. Jawaban AI di Google belum kami uji.
- **Apa yang gratis?** Langkah pertama: kami temukan klinik Anda, Anda cek benar atau tidak. Audit 10 pertanyaannya Rp99.000, dan harga di ringkasan pesanan berlaku 30 hari.
- **Perlu data pasien?** Tidak. Kami hanya memakai informasi publik tentang klinik Anda.
- **Berapa lama?** Cek klinik biasanya kurang dari 10 detik. Laporan dikirim ke email setelah Anda menyetujui pertanyaannya. `[KONFIRMASI: waktu kirim]`
- **Klinik saya punya beberapa cabang.** Satu audit untuk satu fokus: seluruh brand, satu cabang, atau satu layanan.
- **Aman dari sisi aturan iklan kesehatan?** Nuave bukan iklan. Laporannya untuk internal klinik, dan sarannya soal informasi faktual seperti jadwal, layanan, dan lokasi.
- **Kalau ada masalah?** Tulis ke support@nuave.ai. `[KONFIRMASI S5: refund]`

### 9. Final CTA

- **H2:** Tahu jawabannya sebelum pasien bertanya.
- **Line:** Mulai dari website atau Instagram klinik Anda.
- **Form, then:** Rp99.000 sekali bayar. Bayar setelah klinik Anda kami temukan.

## 7. Facts and their limits

| Fact on the page | Source | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| 47% of patients have used a tool like ChatGPT or Google's AI Overviews to research a provider (up from 31% nine months earlier); AI shapes choice more than Google search or a doctor's referral | rater8, *2026 Patient Choice Report*, 992 US adults, April 2026, reported by Medical Economics | US data. rater8 sells online reputation services, so it is not a neutral source. Label it "survei di AS". |
| 40 million people ask ChatGPT health questions every day | OpenAI, *AI as a Healthcare Ally*, January 2026, via Medical Economics | Global, not Indonesia |
| Google shows AI answers in Indonesia (2024), and AI Mode works in Bahasa Indonesia (2025) | Google Indonesia blog, 5 September 2024 and 9 September 2025 | |

The rater8 press release (3 June 2026) also says 60% trusted an AI summary without checking further, and 66% had met wrong provider information from AI. These are strong but come only from the vendor's own release. They are left off the page unless Yasir wants them. "Wrong information" would be a good second line for the blind-spot section.

## 8. Conversion checks kept from the growth plan

- **Price before any scrolling:** the hero microcopy says Rp99.000, and that you pay after we find the clinic.
- **An honest free step:** nothing promises a free AI result.
- **One action:** the same button and form in the hero, the price card and the final section, plus the bottom bar on phones.
- **Proof early:** the report card sits in the hero, and the report section comes around the middle.
- **Objections answered:** in nine FAQ answers instead of across the page.
- **First test:** v2 (light, about 9 screens) against v1 (long, about 22), read by channel. This is the existing long-versus-short test with a clear short version.
