S2AI jadi rujukan
Job: Prove the shift with three numbers.
Visual: Three soft number cards, each with its scope (di AS, di dunia, di Indonesia); one source line.
Conversion: Belief before the problem; no CTA.
Open question: rater8 is a vendor survey (US); keep the 'survei di AS' label.
S3Titik buta
Job: AI names a few clinics; you can't see if you're one.
Visual: Side by side: the patient's AI answer (3 names) vs three clinic tiles (Google Analytics, Statistik Instagram, Google Maps), each 'Belum ada data jawaban AI'. Text labels only, no brand UI.
Conversion: Creates the need without fear; no CTA.
S4Cara kerja
Job: Three steps; what is free, what the owner checks after paying.
Visual: Desktop: three cards with small screen sketches (website → clinic found, 10 questions, report email). Mobile: three numbered rows.
Conversion: Tags 'Gratis' and 'Rp99.000' keep the free step honest.
S5Laporan
Job: Show what you get, in the real format.
Visual: Report in four tabs (Skor · Jawaban AI · Klinik lain · Perbaikan); tap swaps the panel and the grey line. Exact labels kept. All four states are drawn beside the mobile board.
Conversion: Proof at mid-page; the sticky bar shows here.
S6Bukti, bukan janji
Job: Trust without testimonials.
Visual: Four soft cards, icon + 1–2 word title + one line.
Conversion: Candour replaces social proof.
S7Harga
Job: Price, anchor, act.
Visual: One centred price card: checks, reason line, the form, payment chips (names only).
Conversion: The card holds the form, so the reader acts where they decide.
Open question: Refund line [KONFIRMASI S5] stays out of the card until approved.
S8FAQ
Job: Answer objections on demand.
Visual: Nine rows, first open; '+' expands (one open at a time). Anchor #faq.
Conversion: Depth hidden until asked for.
Open question: 'Berapa lama?' delivery time [KONFIRMASI]; 'Kalau ada masalah?' refund line [KONFIRMASI S5]; regulation answer: founder checks wording.
S9Final CTA
Job: Look ahead, act.
Visual: Soft band: headline, line, the form, price micro, founder contact line.
Conversion: Same form component; a human name before leaving.
Open question: Founder contact line: Yasir to confirm wording.
FFooter
Job: Links and legal.
Visual: Logo, four links, ©.
Conversion: FAQ link → #faq.
CSticky bar
Job: keep the one action in reach.
Visual: 64px white bar: "Gratis dulu · Audit Rp99.000" + dark "Cek klinik saya"; padding-bottom env(safe-area-inset-bottom).
Conversion: shows after the hero form leaves view; hides while the hero, price-card or final form is on screen, or the keyboard is open. Tap scrolls to the nearest form below (price card), else the final form.
