Job: One patient question, two answers: ChatGPT and Google AI Overview both answer, and each names only a few clinics. Speaks to the marketer asking how to appear in Google AI Overview.
Visual: Side by side (desktop 1000px) / stacked (mobile). Same question word for word in both: 'rekomendasi klinik behel di Depok yang bisa dicicil'. Left: ChatGPT chat naming Klinik A, B, C. Right: a Google results page drawn in boxes ('Google' text mark, search bar, tabs), a labelled 'AI Overview' box with two small source chips naming Klinik A, D, E; both end in the dashed 'Klinik Anda?'. Two greyed results below (klinik-a.contoh, direktori.contoh). Different lists on purpose: two places, two lists. Text labels only, no logos. Replaces the old 'Yang dilihat klinik' picture.
Conversion: Creates the need without fear; no CTA. The caption keeps the honesty rule: 'Google tidak selalu menampilkan AI Overview.'
Founder question: OK to draw a recognisable Google results page and ChatGPT chat (text labels, no logos) in production?
