- Jabodetabek is the working market umbrella. The first raw report uses one
  exact Depok/Margonda branch, and the final launch geography is not settled.
- The working message is competitive discovery: show where a clinic appears,
  which competitors appear, what public information differs, and what to fix.
- The current founding offer is a Full AI Visibility Audit for Rp149,000.
- Nuave has zero paying v2 customers.
- EXP-R1 completed five OpenAI web-search observations and five Gemini
  Developer API Free observations for Sozo Dental Depok. Gemini mentioned Sozo
  only for the branded question and returned a conflicting branch address.
  ChatGPT Free remains unobserved because this workspace cannot access it.
- A detailed ten-clinic methodology package exists under
  `experiments/EXP-001/`, but no AI observations have been collected.
- The local landing page now preserves the previous Nuave website as a visual
  and interaction baseline. Its copy still describes the earlier free-audit
  platform and requires a later content pass for the current offer.

## What is not known

- Whether clinic owners consider the problem urgent.
- Whether the report is better than asking a chatbot casually.
- Whether owners will pay Rp149,000 without a ranking guarantee.
- Which findings owners consider useful or actionable.
- Which acquisition channel can work economically.
- How much repetition or automation the audit will eventually require.

## Do now

2. Revise any language that requires too much explanation.

4. Ask what is useful, confusing, missing, or unbelievable.

## Not now

- dashboard, accounts, or subscription monitoring;
- generalized multi-provider architecture;
- automated scoring or a composite visibility score;
- multi-city or multi-vertical expansion;
- cold paid-ad scaling;
- extensive operational dashboards and weekly reporting without activity; or
- a statistically representative market study.
