# protocol-v4 clean Stage 1

Status: frozen preparation for one authorized screen; no production use.

This version carries protocol-v3's buyer-language guidance, ten-request measurement requirements, six unnamed/four named composition, named and unnamed comparisons, thresholds, and the same eight fixture contents. The only changes are execution and provenance: separate planner and realizer schemas/instructions, pre-request immutable attempt records, stage validators, and a clean result namespace. The v3 generated S/P results remain historical diagnostic evidence and are never read by this package.

Stage 1: eight S calls and eight P planner→realizer sequences; maximum 24 calls. No retries, repairs, ranking, replacement, or confirmation runs. Fixture inputs are screening material, not fresh held-out confirmation.

Review gates remain: at least 7/10 Keep, at least 9/10 Keep-or-Light, and separate human checks for standalone context, grounding, identity leakage, measurement fidelity, repetition, and old-measurement crosswalk. Shape/composition checks do not prove semantic validity.
