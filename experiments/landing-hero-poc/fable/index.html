Fable · hero concept

Idea: the patient's chat is the hero object. Three fictional clinics and one empty dashed slot show the gap; the floating report card shows what Nuave finds.

From Airbnb's host pages: a tight 60/64 semibold headline at -0.035em, one quiet 20px line, then only the form and the object; a 72px nav with one outline button.

From Airbnb's search bar and chips: field and button share one 68px bar on desktop; pill tabs like their category chips; 24px cards with one soft shadow and no borders; the report card floats over the stage like their map tooltip.

Nuave kept: Geist, white canvas, ink neutrals, cream only as the demo stage and the patient's bubble, violet once (the check button), ink report beads, the empty kedai as "Klinik Anda?".

Mobile: centred headline and line, stacked field and button; at 360×640 the price line ends at 488 and the tab row starts at the fold.
